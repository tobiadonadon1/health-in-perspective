Xeyo and WHOOP

Xeyo (xeyo.ai) is a web health dashboard that puts a member's WHOOP data next to their nutrition, mood, body measurements and blood work, and explains each reading against the member's own last 30 days. These are screens from the app with sample data.

  • read:recovery and read:cycles: recovery, HRV, resting heart rate and strain on the dashboard, each against the member's own baseline.
  • read:sleep: last night's sleep and stages on the Sleep page.
  • offline: keeps the dashboard current without reconnecting. Members can disconnect at any time, which revokes the token.
