Data-use notice · Updated 23 October 2026

This experimental web application helps you organize your own wearable measurements, nutrition entries and health records. It is not a medical diagnostic service. The operator and contact is Tobia Donadon: tobia@donadon.com.

What you choose to store

Your account email and profile name; meals and nutrition facts you save; blood-test values, dates, units, laboratory ranges and notes you enter; and reports or reference photos you upload. Your onboarding choices include your age, gender, display preference and optional participation in peer comparisons. Your display can use an approved AI portrait based on a private uploaded photo, a digital Vitruvian figure or statistics alone. Neither a photo nor an illustration measures body composition.

Connecting WHOOP

You can authorize the application through WHOOP’s own sign-in screen. With your permission, the application imports available recovery and sleep measurements, including heart rate, HRV, oxygen saturation, sleep duration, respiratory rate and recovery score. Availability depends on your device and WHOOP account. We do not receive your WHOOP password. Authorization tokens are encrypted on the server. Choose Disconnect in Integrations to revoke access. Imported measurements remain in your records unless you request their removal.

Storage and access

Account services, data and private files are hosted by Supabase in the United States (US East). Database ownership rules restrict access to your account. File downloads use expiring links. The web interface is hosted using OpenAI Sites. The operator and hosting providers may access data where needed to operate, maintain or support the service; private does not mean end-to-end encrypted.

Optional food analysis

Manual meal entry does not require AI. You can connect your own OpenAI API key in Profile. It is encrypted on our server, never returned to the browser, and can be replaced or removed. Removing it from this app does not revoke it at OpenAI. Provider usage is billed to your own OpenAI account. When you request analysis, your meal descriptions, dish or label images and corrections are sent to OpenAI for nutrition estimation, or, if you have not stored an OpenAI nutrition key, to the AI provider you connected in Profile. Voice recordings are sent to OpenAI for transcription. Photo-based portions and nutrition values are estimates. Submitted meal photos and recordings are processed transiently and are not stored in your journal; estimated food entries are saved automatically after analysis. We request that OpenAI not store the model response, but the provider may retain service or abuse-monitoring logs under its own policies. Review saved estimates and use Edit or Undo to correct them. These features do not infer blood glucose, inflammation or body fat. Health reports are not sent for AI analysis. Reference photos are sent only when you request portrait generation, as described below.

Optional chatbot

In Chatbot you can connect OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude), Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Mistral, Groq, xAI (Grok) or DeepSeek with your own API key and model. OpenRouter forwards requests to the model vendor you select; that vendor's policies also apply. Keys are encrypted on our server and are not returned to the browser. Messages and recent conversation history for the selected provider and model are sent to that provider when you press Send. If you enable “Share my 30-day reading averages”, the request also includes averages grouped by measurement, source and unit; it excludes your name, age, gender, photos and lab reports. Conversations are saved privately in Xeyo, included in your data export, and can be cleared in Chatbot. Clearing Xeyo history does not erase data retained by the provider. Disconnecting removes the stored key; revoke the key at your provider to invalidate it there. Providers apply their own retention and billing terms. An AI response is not medical advice or a diagnosis.

Your private health journal

Check-ins are stored privately with the date you choose. You can edit or delete notes and export them from Profile. When you choose Reflect with my AI, the selected note is sent to your Chatbot provider, or to OpenAI if you have only connected your nutrition/portrait key. If you tick Include my recent wearable trends, the request also includes latest readings and seven- and thirty-day averages grouped by metric, unit and source, with dates and recorded-day counts. It excludes photos, lab reports, your identity and other journal entries. The AI reflection is stored alongside the note and clearly labelled as AI text. Editing a note removes its previous reflection; deleting a note removes both. AI output can be wrong and is not a diagnosis. Provider retention and billing terms apply.

Optional peer comparisons

With your explicit opt-in, your last 30 days of readings contribute to aggregate comparisons among participating Xeyo members in your age band. Each participant needs at least 7 recorded UTC days, and results are shown only with at least 20 other qualifying people using the same measurement, unit and source. Names, photos and individual peer readings are not disclosed. The comparison is not representative of the general population and does not assign a health score. Age bands are 18–29, 30–39, 40–49, 50–59, 60–69, 70–79 and 80+. Gender is not used for ranking. You can withdraw in Profile; future queries then exclude your readings. Existing viewed results cannot be recalled. Confirm your age yearly to stay eligible.

Health export imports

Apple Health and Fitbit/Google Health export files are read locally in your browser. Only supported measurements from the last 90 days are uploaded after you confirm the import. Original archives are not uploaded or sent to AI. Imported data is labeled as a file import and retains its source. Vital signs use daily averages; weight and body composition use the latest value for each source and day. File imports do not enable automatic synchronization.

Retention, export and removal

Saved entries are retained in your account until removed. You can delete individual meals, blood results and uploaded records, and export your structured data from Profile. For complete account erasure or removal of imported wearable data, contact tobia@donadon.com using your registered email. Account deletion is currently handled by the operator. Infrastructure backups, where enabled, follow the hosting provider’s retention cycles.

Sessions and service logs

The application stores sign-in session information in your browser and temporary authentication-flow state in session storage. Hosting and authentication services keep operational logs used to troubleshoot and protect the service. This build does not include advertising trackers or sell health data.

Availability and choices

WHOOP requires a compatible account and your consent. Apple Health and Fitbit file imports are available as snapshots. Automatic Apple Watch and Fitbit synchronization is not active in this release. You can use nutrition and records without connecting a wearable. Contact the operator for questions, corrections, export or deletion requests. This experiment is intended for adults.

Personal portrait generation

When you select Generate in the portrait studio, your chosen reference photo and the Xeyo style image are sent to OpenAI using your personal API key. Image generation is billed by OpenAI to your API account. The original photo and generated portraits are stored privately; you review and approve a render before it appears on your dashboard. This is an illustrative image, not a 3D scan or a measurement of body composition. Deleting the original reference record also removes its generated portraits.
