# Digital Vitruvian figures

Generated for Xeyo on 23 October 2026 with the built-in image generation tool. These are transparent raster assets styled as neutral, softly lit 3D mannequins, not interactive 3D models or body measurements. Both use bald, minimally defined heads and restrained anatomy, without the circle or square.

Production assets:
- `../public/images/vitruvian-man-digital.png`
- `../public/images/vitruvian-woman-digital.png`

The existing `digital` profile value selects this representation. The saved `gender` value selects the female artwork when it is `woman`; other values use the male artwork. Onboarding previews the corresponding figure before saving. No profile migration is required. The legacy WebGL bust, model and Three.js dependency were removed. The superseded classical circle-and-square drawing was removed from the production bundle.

## Final prompt — man

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a minimalist neutral digital Vitruvian display mannequin for the Xeyo health dashboard. NOT a character, NOT a realistic person. Smooth bald featureless head, no hair, no identifiable face, no eyes or mouth details; only an extremely subtle nose plane. A fully opaque, anatomically simplified mannequin surface from neck to feet, seamless, smooth and gender-neutral in anatomical detail, with no clothing seams. Quiet soft rounded contours, no defined musculature, no sculpted abs, no exaggerated curves or anatomical details. Materials: softly translucent-looking but opaque opaline ivory polymer with very pale neutral sage shadows and a whisper of warm peach light on edges. Modern clean dimensional 3D render, gentle diffused studio light, restrained satin highlights, soft focus at the outer edges. An abstract medical interface figure, not a fashion model, robot, sculpture drawing, or superhero. Full figure facing forward in the Vitruvian configuration: TWO symmetrical pairs of arms, one horizontal and one diagonal upward; TWO pairs of legs, one together and one diagonal outward. Secondary limb positions slightly more translucent and softly faded. All fingertips and feet visible. Complete subject centered on a square canvas occupying about 88 percent, generous breathing room. Genuinely transparent background. No circle, no square, no frame, no geometry, no text, no numbers, no base, no floor, no cast shadow behind it. Variant: understated masculine overall proportions, slightly wider straight shoulders and a straight torso, still an ordinary slender neutral mannequin with no athletic muscle definition.

## Final prompt — woman

The generated male asset was supplied as the style reference.

Use case: style-transfer. Create the matching female variant of this abstract pearl-white digital display mannequin for a health app. Preserve the reference's simple bald head, minimal featureless face, restrained ivory and pale sage colors, soft 3D lighting, full Vitruvian pose with two pairs of arms and two pairs of legs, and transparent background. The mannequin is wearing a completely opaque smooth ivory full-length unitard covering the entire body from neck to wrists and ankles. Keep a straight, very minimally shaped torso without anatomical detail, no visible muscle definition, and ordinary slender limbs. Only a slight reduction in shoulder width and slightly softer overall proportions distinguish this variant. It should have almost no individual personality, just a neutral digital human outline. No realistic face, no hair, no curves emphasised, no garment decoration, no geometry, no circle, no square, no text, no floor, no background. Complete hands and feet inside the frame. Clean satin opaline material, not a drawing.
